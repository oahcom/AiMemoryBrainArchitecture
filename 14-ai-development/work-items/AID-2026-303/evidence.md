**Repository:** `oahcom/AiMemoryBrainArchitecture`

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Attribution:** user-authorized operator (delegated by account owner)

## 1. Objective

Repair every measured source revision pointer failure without changing the
scanner's decision logic, and integrate the unchanged scanner into the existing
required Repository hygiene job as a hard gate.

Two earlier positions are withdrawn:

- The two `memory-brain-guide.md` mirrors are no longer left as unresolved
  debt. The Owner adjudicated `03-memory-brain/memory-brain-overview.md` as
  their canonical source.
- The exact-failure-set ratchet is withdrawn. Issue #1462 demonstrates that
  `gate_ratchet.py` reads its baseline from the reviewed worktree, so the
  reviewed branch can widen its own exemption set and the gate reports green
  while the scanner fails. A ratchet with a worktree-controlled baseline is
  not a gate.

## 5. Workflow integration

`scripts/ci/baselines/source-revision-pointers.txt` is deleted. The two ratchet
steps added by the previous revision are removed.

The existing `.github/workflows/repository-hygiene.yml` job keeps the
`Self-check source revision pointer verification` step and gains one step
immediately after it:

- `Check source revision pointers (hard gate)` runs
  `python3 scripts/check_source_revision_pointers.py` directly.

No `gate_ratchet.py`, no `sed` filter, no baseline file, no `|| true`. The
scanner's exit code is the step result.

No new workflow, job, required context, branch-protection setting, scanner
decision rule, or scanner exit-code behavior is introduced.

The `scripts/check_source_revision_pointers.py` entry remains absent from
`scripts/ci/baselines/gate-wiring.txt`; the gate is now wired, so it must not be
registered as unwired.
