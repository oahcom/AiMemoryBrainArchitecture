## 4. The four non-`FIXED` decisions

### 4.1 `AID-2026-261` — 2 declarations, `FORMALLY_RETIRED`

`session_evidence[0]` declared `executed_prompt_sha256: 000…0` and
`executed_prompt_bytes: 0` against `prompt-v1.md`, which was never committed.
The declaration itself records the reason (`prompt_status: NOT_RECORDED`,
`executed_prompt_provenance: NOT_VERIFIED`, `session_reference_status:
NOT_RECORDED`) and its trailing comment called the two values sentinels.

They cannot be recomputed — there is no target file — and they cannot stay:
the pin checker reads a non-empty digest as a pin, so both surfaced as
`PATH_MISSING` on every run. The work-item schema
(`14-ai-development/work-items/work-item-manifest.schema.yaml`) requires only
`role`, `issue_refs`, `implementing_ai_session` and `session_evidence_ref` in
a `session_evidence` item, so removing the two sentinel values keeps the
record schema-valid. The two fields were deleted; `executed_prompt_snapshot_file`
and both status fields are kept, and the comment now records the retirement.
No digest was invented.

### 4.2 `AID-2026-100` `authored_indexes[1]` — 3 declarations, `FORMALLY_RETIRED`

The entry's own `authoritative_source` says main carries the restored
`09-security-governance/README.md` through AID-2026-109, that this
registration "no longer authors this file and makes no provenance claim about
it". Keeping three stale blob claims under that text would assert exactly the
provenance the entry disclaims, and repinning them to main would attribute
AID-2026-109's bytes to AID-2026-100. The three `*_blob` claims were therefore
removed with that declaration as the basis; the entry, its paths, its
`provenance: SUPERSEDED_BY_MAIN` and its status remain.

`authored_indexes[0]` is different: it is a live `AUTHORED_INDEX` entry, so its
three blobs were repinned to the candidate tree (`FIXED`).

## 5. Ratchet removal

`scripts/ci/gate_ratchet.py` fails in both directions: a new item is a
regression, and a baseline item that stops failing is a stale baseline. The
baseline `scripts/ci/baselines/manifest-blob-pins.txt` recorded the 89
mismatches and 2 missing paths, so **repairing a declared value necessarily
turned the step red**. Data repair and a preserved ratchet are mutually
exclusive here.

The change is therefore:

- `scripts/ci/baselines/manifest-blob-pins.txt` deleted;
- the `Ratchet manifest blob pins` step replaced by a direct
  `python3 scripts/ci/check_manifest_blob_pins.py --commit HEAD`, whose exit
  status is the gate: `1` on any mismatch or missing path, `2` on any
  incomplete declaration, `0` only when every pin verifies. No `status > 1`
  relaxation, no replacement baseline, no allowlist.

**Precondition for deleting the baseline**: all 93 declarations have a
terminal state, and the re-run on the final head reports
`mismatch=0 / path_missing=0 / incomplete=0` — the same numbers the deleted
baseline can no longer absorb. Both runs are pasted in §6.
