# Phase 3.8 — I Commit Authorization Receipt and Revocation Sequencer Contract

- Owner role: I — Security, Identity and Governance
- Status: **I-role proposal; exact Owner approval, crypto profile and conformance OPEN**
- Related: Issue #25; A Phase 3.8.1 contract-surface addendum
- English and Chinese are normative peers.

## 1. I authority boundary

I owns authenticated principal/workload/delegation trust, PolicyDomain, current authorization, PermissionEpoch, revocation ordering, issuer/key status and authorization terminal proof.

I does not own D ForgetFence or D PublicationParticipantRef, Storage durability/visibility, E compiler lifecycle, C ParticipantIntentRef or H transport serialization.

I authorization is necessary but never sufficient for visibility. D's current publication participant/fence and Storage's exact durable participant/outcome are independent conjunctive requirements.

## 2. Authorization subject and immutable publication binding

For each derivative publication, I evaluates:
- authenticated principal, actor, workload and delegation chain;
- issuer identity and issuer key ID/version/status;
- action = derivative publication at the named final visibility gate;
- resource = exact DerivedArtifactRef/version/content digest;
- purpose, audience, Scope, Environment and OwnerBoundary;
- PolicyDomainRef and current I policy/revocation/registry state;
- exact PublicationOperationId and canonical PublicationTupleDigest;
- exact D PublicationParticipantRef and D fence/lineage proof reference;
- exact StorageVisibilityParticipantRef and Storage owner/visibility boundary;
- producer work identity, class/version, AttemptId/LeaseId/LeaseEpoch;
- dependency receipt and complete dependency-set digest.

Payload-supplied identity, scope, permission epoch, producer or lineage values are untrusted assertions until verified against authenticated and authoritative records.

## 3. Single-use sequencer operation

I exposes a logical operation `ConsumeCommitAuthorization` (name is provisional) in the authoritative PolicyDomain sequencer.

Input:
- authenticated caller context;
- exact immutable publication tuple digest;
- exact D PublicationParticipantRef;
- exact durable StorageVisibilityParticipantRef;
- action/resource/purpose/audience/Scope/Environment/OwnerBoundary;
- current policy and issuer/key context.

The sequencer atomically orders this consumption against revocation and policy/registry changes in the same PolicyDomain.

Results:
- COMMIT_WON: this exact publication has won the I authorization/revocation ordering race. I persists an unresolved authorization participant and holds later applicable revocation until Storage terminal outcome.
- REVOCATION_WON: applicable revocation/policy change linearized first. No Storage visibility commit is permitted.
- UNKNOWN / IN_DOUBT: ordering or persistence cannot be proven. I participant/revocation remain held; no visibility.

COMMIT_WON is not a general bearer token, not a detached ALLOW snapshot and not a standalone Storage write permit.

## 4. Receipt and proof model

The protocol distinguishes:
1. `CommitAuthorizationRequest`: caller input, not authority evidence.
2. `CommitAuthorizationReceipt`: I-signed durable sequencer result for one single-use consumption.
3. `ITerminalProof`: I-signed closeout proof after authoritative Storage outcome.

Minimum CommitAuthorizationReceipt binding:
- authorization_receipt_ref and unique nonce;
- PolicyDomainRef, policy version and I-local PermissionEpoch;
- issuer key ID/version/status reference;
- authenticated subject/actor/workload and delegation-chain digest;
- action, exact artifact/version/content digest, purpose, audience, Scope, Environment;
- OwnerBoundary and final visibility gate identity;
- PublicationOperationId and PublicationTupleDigest;
- exact D PublicationParticipantRef and exact StorageVisibilityParticipantRef;
- I-local sequencer position and consumption result COMMIT_WON / REVOCATION_WON / UNKNOWN;
- issued_at, consumed_at, first-consumption accept-before (if used);
- canonicalization profile, signature profile and signature.

The receipt's consumption result is generated by I's sequencer, never caller-selected. If an implementation uses a pre-issued authorization token before sequencer consumption, that token is a distinct nominal type and cannot be confused with the consumed receipt.

ITerminalProof binds the exact receipt, PolicyDomain, publication tuple, D/Storage participant refs and exact authoritative Storage outcome digest (COMMITTED_VISIBLE or NO_PUBLICATION). UNKNOWN never yields ITerminalProof.

## 5. Revocation ordering

- Revocation/policy change first: ConsumeCommitAuthorization returns REVOCATION_WON; no visibility.
- Consumption COMMIT_WON first: I records unresolved participant; later applicable revocation waits for Storage terminal outcome.
- Storage COMMITTED_VISIBLE: I closes the exact participant; later revocation applies to future/current-use eligibility according to policy but does not rewrite publication history.
- Storage NO_PUBLICATION: I closes as safely not published; held revocation may advance.
- UNKNOWN/IN_DOUBT: keep participant and affected revocation unresolved.

No timeout, missing row, lease expiry, process death, key cache, stale replica or age-based cleanup releases the I gate.

## 6. Key, replay and anti-rollback requirements

Before accepting a request, I must validate issuer/key status and delegation chain under current policy. Exact rules remain I-owned decisions, but the implementation contract must specify:
- key ID/version and status at issuance and verification;
- rotation, revocation, compromise and historical signature validation;
- audience and OwnerBoundary binding;
- nonce uniqueness and single-consumption persistence;
- replay rejection across different ArtifactRef/version, Scope, Attempt, Storage participant or tuple digest;
- policy/PermissionEpoch rollback detection within the PolicyDomain;
- canonical digest and signature algorithm/profile version;
- retention of receipts/nonces beyond every retry, restore and audit horizon;
- fail-closed behavior when issuer status, registry continuity or sequencer position is unknown.

An expired accept-before blocks first consumption only. It must not silently terminalize or release an already consumed COMMIT_WON participant.

## 7. Storage final-boundary proof semantics

Storage must validate an I terminal authorization proof whose meaning remains valid at the actual visibility linearization point. A detached signed ALLOW snapshot or unconsumed token is insufficient.

I and Storage must define how Storage can verify:
- proof issuer and key status;
- exact PolicyDomain and currentness claim;
- exact D participant and Storage participant;
- exact publication tuple digest;
- sequencer COMMIT_WON result and unresolved participant status;
- no intervening revocation has won before consumption.

If Storage cannot independently validate a proof at commit, a jointly approved online validation or cryptographically verifiable sequencer proof protocol is required. The protocol cannot compare PermissionEpoch numerically with D or Storage positions.

## 8. Closeout and recovery

I closes only after it reads authoritative Storage COMMITTED_VISIBLE or NO_PUBLICATION for the same publication operation, tuple digest and exact participant refs. It then persists ITerminalProof and releases the held revocation as appropriate.

If Storage outcome is missing, contradictory or UNKNOWN, I remains IN_DOUBT. Restore must reconcile I sequencer continuity, policy/registry/key status, in-flight participants and Storage outcome before authorizing current use. Historical COMMIT_WON is not sufficient to re-enable serving after restore; current policy is re-evaluated.

## 9. Required I tests

- Revocation-first vs receipt consumption.
- COMMIT_WON then revocation pending.
- Duplicate nonce / same request replay.
- Same nonce with changed tuple digest.
- Different ArtifactRef/version, Scope, Attempt or Storage participant replay.
- Issuer key rotation/revocation/compromise.
- PolicyDomain epoch rollback or sequencer gap.
- Lost response after COMMIT_WON.
- Storage outcome COMMITTED_VISIBLE / NO_PUBLICATION / UNKNOWN.
- Restore with missing or contradictory Storage outcome.
- Expired first-consumption token vs already consumed COMMIT_WON participant.

## 10. Open I decisions

Exact crypto/signature suite, canonical wire serialization, issuer key-status distribution, revocation propagation guarantees, nonce/receipt retention, PolicyDomain sequencer implementation and Storage proof verification are OPEN and require I/H/Storage decisions.

## 11. 中文规范

### 11.1 I 权威边界
I 管理认证主体/工作负载/Delegation Trust、PolicyDomain、当前授权、PermissionEpoch、撤权顺序、Issuer/Key Status 和授权终态证明。I 不拥有 D ForgetFence/PublicationParticipantRef、Storage 持久化/可见性、E Compiler Lifecycle、C ParticipantIntentRef 或 H Transport Serialization。I Authorization 是必要条件，但绝不是 Visibility 的充分条件。

### 11.2 单次 Sequencer Consumption
I 在权威 PolicyDomain Sequencer 中提供逻辑操作 ConsumeCommitAuthorization。输入必须绑定不可变 Publication Tuple Digest、精确 D PublicationParticipantRef、精确且已持久化的 StorageVisibilityParticipantRef，以及认证主体、Action/Resource/Purpose/Audience/Scope/Environment/OwnerBoundary。Sequencer 与同 PolicyDomain 的 Revocation/Policy/Registry Change 原子排序。

COMMIT_WON 表示该精确 Publication 赢得 I 授权/撤权排序竞态；I 持久化未解决 Participant，并让后续适用 Revocation 等待 Storage Terminal Outcome。REVOCATION_WON 阻断 Visibility。UNKNOWN/IN_DOUBT 保持 Gate 开放。COMMIT_WON 不是通用 Bearer Token、Detached ALLOW Snapshot 或独立 Storage Write Permit。

### 11.3 Receipt 类型
必须区分 CommitAuthorizationRequest、CommitAuthorizationReceipt 和 ITerminalProof。Receipt 至少绑定唯一 Nonce、PolicyDomain/Policy Version/PermissionEpoch、Issuer Key ID/Version/Status、Subject/Actor/Workload/Delegation Digest、Action/Artifact/Version/Digest、Purpose/Audience/Scope/Environment/OwnerBoundary/Final Gate、PublicationOperationId/Tuple Digest、精确 D/Storage Participant、I-local Sequencer Position、消费结果、时间、Canonicalization/Signature Profile。消费结果只能由 I Sequencer 产生，不得由调用方选择。

### 11.4 撤权顺序
Revocation-first 返回 REVOCATION_WON。COMMIT_WON-first 创建未解决 Participant，并保持后续 Revocation Pending，直到 Storage COMMITTED_VISIBLE 或 NO_PUBLICATION。UNKNOWN 不得释放 Gate。超时、行缺失、Lease 过期、进程死亡、Key Cache、陈旧 Replica 或对象年龄都不能证明终态。

### 11.5 Key / Replay / Anti-rollback
I 必须明确 Key Rotation/Revocation/Compromise、Audience/OwnerBoundary、Nonce 唯一消费、跨 Artifact/Version/Scope/Attempt/Storage Participant/Tuple 的 Replay 防护、PolicyDomain Rollback/Sequencer Gap、Canonical Digest/Signature Profile、Receipt Retention 和 Unknown 时 Fail-closed。accept-before 只限制首次消费，不能让已消费 COMMIT_WON 自动过期释放。

### 11.6 Storage 最终验证
Storage 必须在真实 Visibility Linearization Point 验证 I Terminal Authorization Proof 的签发者/Key Status、PolicyDomain/Currentness、精确 D/Storage Participant、Tuple Digest、COMMIT_WON 及未解决 Participant 状态。Detached ALLOW Snapshot 不足。若无法独立验证，必须采用经 I/Storage 批准的在线验证或可验证 Sequencer Proof。禁止跨域比较 PermissionEpoch。

### 11.7 恢复与未决事项
I 仅在读取同一 Publication Operation/Tuple/Participant 的权威 Storage Outcome 后签发 ITerminalProof。Restore 必须核对 Sequencer Continuity、Policy/Registry/Key Status、In-flight Participant 和 Storage Outcome。密码算法、Wire Canonicalization、Key Status Distribution、Revocation Propagation、Nonce Retention、Sequencer Implementation 和 Storage Proof Verification 仍 OPEN。
