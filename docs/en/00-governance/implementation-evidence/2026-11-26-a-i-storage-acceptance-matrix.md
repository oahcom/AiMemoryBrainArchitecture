## Fact vocabulary — non-substitution rule

- `Received`: transport/receiver fact; not Accepted.
- `Delivered`: delivery transport/state fact; not proof of receiver acceptance.
- `Accepted`: authoritative C/H evidence fact; not G Applied.
- `Applied`: G's complete predicate and durable evidence; not model comprehension or use.
- `CanonicalCommitted`: D canonical transaction fact; not indexed.
- `Indexed`: F index consumer fact; not proof of complete scan coverage.
- `scanned_through`: contiguous scan-coverage proof; not last-visible record cursor.

No row may infer one fact from another without an explicit normative transition contract.
