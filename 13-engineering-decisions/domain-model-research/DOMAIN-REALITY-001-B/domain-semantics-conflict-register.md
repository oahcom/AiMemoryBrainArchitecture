## 2. Conflict resolution protocol / 冲突解决流程

1. Pin the exact conflicting artifact branch/path/Blob and, where available, commit SHA.
2. Quote the exact semantic clauses and state why they are incompatible (not merely differently worded).
3. Identify the sole authority for each clause; do not assign a cross-owner authority by convenience.
4. Mark affected contract/schema/gate and safe interim behavior (normally fail closed / defer).
5. Obtain explicit Owner-authored decision with exact version and exceptions.
6. Update only B-owned synthesis; never edit another Owner's normative file.
7. Re-run bilingual parity and invariant validation; retain superseded evidence and record explicit supersession.
