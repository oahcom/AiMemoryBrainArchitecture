## Required change record
ID; target baseline/redline/gate/authority; current and proposed version; requester; rationale; alternatives; impact analysis; affected owners and responses; security/privacy/Forget impact; compatibility/migration/rollback; verification plan; approval evidence; effective date; expiry if temporary; supersession links.

Change status uses PROPOSED, APPROVED, ACTIVE, SUPERSEDED, WAIVED. APPROVED requires competent authority evidence. ACTIVE requires effective date and publication. SUPERSEDED preserves prior content. WAIVED is limited to exact scope and time. Never overwrite history; create a new row and version.
