## Status semantics
- PROPOSED: submitted, not approved and not effective.
- APPROVED: explicit approval recorded by competent authority; may have future effective date.
- ACTIVE: approved and effective for the stated scope/date.
- SUPERSEDED: replaced by a later approved version; retained for history.
- WAIVED: a specific requirement has an approved, scoped, time-limited exception; underlying baseline remains recorded.

No row may be advanced without approval evidence, approver identity/authority, exact artifact version and date. Superseded rows are never deleted. A WAIVED entry must link the waiver record and expiry. Status is not inferred from merge, commit, author label or module adoption.
