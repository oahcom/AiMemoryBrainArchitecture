## Required record fields
Every violation record must include unique ID, redline ID/version, detection date, exact artifact/branch/commit or runtime evidence, affected assets and users, severity/impact, reporter, accountable owner, containment, root cause, remediation, verification evidence, approver, disposition, and links to related ADR/finding/incident.

Disposition states: OPEN, CONTAINED, REMEDIATION_IN_PROGRESS, VERIFIED_CLOSED, ACCEPTED_EXCEPTION, REJECTED. An exception must also be recorded in the baseline-change registry with PGA approval, affected owner concurrence, rationale, exact scope, compensating controls, start/end/expiry, revocation trigger and audit proof.

No module Owner can unilaterally close a project-redline violation. A finding closes only after independent evidence verifies remediation or a valid time-bounded waiver is approved. Preserve all history; never delete closed or superseded records.
